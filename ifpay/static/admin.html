Who may do what

Shop staff sign in with their own IntraForge account and open IntraForge Pay from IntraForge's Apps page. What each person may do is set in IntraForge, by the shop owner, under the organization's Members: give a member the role Pay Cashier (counter: payment QR codes and the shop's receipts), Pay Supervisor (customer wallets, credit requests and approvals, history) or Pay Manager (all of it). The owner (Organization Administrator) holds everything. A change takes effect on the person's next click.

Receipts are stored in this Pay's own files in IntraForge, and Pay's history is copied into IntraForge's audit log.

People known to this Pay

Shop staff appear after they first open Pay; their roles are managed in IntraForge. Customers are this shop's wallet accounts.
